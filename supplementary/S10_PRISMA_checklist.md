# Supplementary Data S10 — PRISMA 2020 checklist

Compiled 1 November 2026 for *Evaluation practice in single-cell cancer genomics*.

**Scope statement.** This is a meta-research review of the evaluation literature and of public
validation-bearing data, not a systematic review of a clinical or biological intervention. Two record
screens were run and are reported in the two-arm flow diagram (`figures/PRISMA_flow.pdf`, counts in
`tables/PRISMA_counts.csv`): Arm 1, a PubMed screen of evaluation-titled records that yields the benchmark
catalogue (Supplementary Data S1, n = 269); Arm 2, a GEO screen that yields the ground-truth catalogue
(Supplementary Data S3, 923 series in 830 study groups). Several PRISMA 2020 items assume a synthesis of
effect estimates extracted from included studies and therefore have no counterpart here. Those items are
marked **Not applicable** with the reason. Items that a systematic review would normally do and that this
review did **not** do are marked **Not done** — they are reported as omissions, not as compliance.

## Summary of non-compliance

Six PRISMA 2020 items are **not done** and are not presented as met: 11 and 18 (risk of bias in included
studies), 14 and 21 (reporting-bias assessment), 15 and 22 (certainty of evidence), and 24a (registration
and protocol). Three further items are **partial**: 6 (a single bibliographic database per arm, no
reference-list hand-search), 8 (the second screening pass is a language-model rater, not a second
independent human reviewer, with no third-reviewer adjudication), and 9 (extraction not independently
duplicated for every record). These are design limitations of a single-author meta-research review, and
they bound how the counts in this paper should be read: they are reproducible lower bounds on what the
abstracts and GEO records state, not appraised estimates of what the underlying studies did.
